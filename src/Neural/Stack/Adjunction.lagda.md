# Transporting the classifier {defines="classifier-adjunction"}

Two adjunctions transport theories along an arrow $\alpha$ of the
base, and it matters to keep them apart. At the level of the *total*
category, the transitions of the [[glued classifier|glued-classifier]]
pull sieves back along the cartesian arrows, and the forcing module's
quantifiers provide a right adjoint — an instantiation of
$f^\star \dashv \forall_f$, recorded first below. The paper's
**Lemma 2.4** (equations 2.24–2.29) lives instead at the level of the
*fibre* classifiers: its $\Omega_\alpha = \lambda_\alpha$ is the
**direct image** of fibre sieves along the transition functor
$F_\alpha$ (the map induced by $F_!$ on subobjects of Yoneda
presheaves, equations 2.10–2.11), and $\tau'_\alpha$ is the
**preimage**. That Galois adjunction holds for *any* $\alpha$; what
the fibration hypothesis buys — exactly as in the paper's proof — is
the *section property*: the counit 2.26 is an equality
$\Omega_\alpha \circ \tau'_\alpha = \mathrm{Id}$, making
$\tau'_\alpha$ a split monomorphism of posets and $\Omega_\alpha$ a
split surjection. Both levels are below, the fibrewise one carrying
the paper's numbering.

## The fibrewise sieve posets

```agda
Sieves-on : (o : ∫F .Ob) → Poset ℓ ℓ
Sieves-on o .Ob = Sieve ∫F o
Sieves-on o ._≤_ S T = S ⊆ T
Sieves-on o .≤-thin = hlevel 1
Sieves-on o .≤-refl h m = m
Sieves-on o .≤-trans p q h m = q h (p h m)
Sieves-on o .≤-antisym p q = ext λ h → Ω-ua (p h) (q h)
```

## The adjunction

The left adjoint is the classifier's transition — pullback along
the cartesian arrow, definitionally — and the right adjoint is the
universal quantifier along it.

```agda
module _ {U V : B.Ob} (α : B.Hom U V) (ξ : Fib V .Ob) where
  private
    cart : ∫F .Hom (U , α ·₀ ξ) (V , ξ)
    cart = ∫hom α (Fib U .id)

  Ω-tr : Monotone (Sieves-on (V , ξ)) (Sieves-on (U , α ·₀ ξ))
  Ω-tr .hom = pullback cart
  Ω-tr .pres-≤ p h m = p (∫F ._∘_ cart h) m

  τ' : Monotone (Sieves-on (U , α ·₀ ξ)) (Sieves-on (V , ξ))
  τ' .hom = ∀[ cart ]
  τ' .pres-≤ {T} {T'} p = ∀[]-adj-→ cart (∀[ cart ] T) T' λ h m →
    p h (∀[]-adj-← cart (∀[ cart ] T) T (λ h' m' → m') h m)

  Ω⊣τ' : Ω-tr ⊣ₚ τ'
  Ω⊣τ' .unit {S} = ∀[]-adj-→ cart S (pullback cart S) (λ h m → m)
  Ω⊣τ' ._⊣ₚ_.counit {T} = ∀[]-adj-← cart (∀[ cart ] T) T (λ h m → m)
```

## The fibrewise transports: Lemma 2.4 proper

The paper's $\Omega_\alpha$ sends a sieve $S'$ on $\xi'$ in the
fibre $F_{U'}$ to the sieve *generated by* the $F_\alpha$-images of
its arrows, on $F_\alpha(\xi')$ in $F_U$; the transport
$\tau'_\alpha$ sends a sieve $T$ to the arrows whose image lies in
$T$ — the paper's "$P \circ F_\alpha$, seen as a sub-sheaf". Image
against preimage is a Galois connection with no hypothesis at all:
equations 2.27–2.29 verbatim.

```agda
fibre-sieves : (W : B.Ob) (ξ : Fib W .Ob) → Poset ℓ ℓ
fibre-sieves W ξ .Ob = Sieve (Fib W) ξ
fibre-sieves W ξ ._≤_ S T = S ⊆ T
fibre-sieves W ξ .≤-thin = hlevel 1
fibre-sieves W ξ .≤-refl h m = m
fibre-sieves W ξ .≤-trans p q h m = q h (p h m)
fibre-sieves W ξ .≤-antisym p q = ext λ h → Ω-ua (p h) (q h)

module _ {U V : B.Ob} (α : B.Hom U V) {ξ' : Fib V .Ob} where
  Ω[_] : Sieve (Fib V) ξ' → Sieve (Fib U) (α ·₀ ξ')
  Ω[ S ] .arrows {η} v = elΩ do
    Σ[ η' ∈ Fib V .Ob ] Σ[ u' ∈ Fib V .Hom η' ξ' ]
      Σ[ w ∈ Fib U .Hom η (α ·₀ η') ]
        ((u' ∈ S) × (v ≡ Fib U ._∘_ (α ·₁ u') w))
  Ω[ S ] .closed {f = v} m w₀ = rec! (λ η' u' w mem eq → inc
    ( η' , u' , Fib U ._∘_ w w₀ , mem
    , ap (λ t → Fib U ._∘_ t w₀) eq ∙ sym (Fib U .assoc (α ·₁ u') w w₀)))
    m

  τ'[_] : Sieve (Fib U) (α ·₀ ξ') → Sieve (Fib V) ξ'
  τ'[ T ] .arrows u' = T .arrows (α ·₁ u')
  τ'[ T ] .closed {f = u'} m w' =
    subst (_∈ T) (sym (F.₁ α .Functor.F-∘ u' w')) (T .closed m (α ·₁ w'))

  Ω-mono : Monotone (fibre-sieves V ξ') (fibre-sieves U (α ·₀ ξ'))
  Ω-mono .hom = Ω[_]
  Ω-mono .pres-≤ p v = rec! λ η' u' w mem eq →
    inc (η' , u' , w , p u' mem , eq)

  τ'-mono : Monotone (fibre-sieves U (α ·₀ ξ')) (fibre-sieves V ξ')
  τ'-mono .hom = τ'[_]
  τ'-mono .pres-≤ p u' m = p (α ·₁ u') m

  Ω-fib⊣τ' : Ω-mono ⊣ₚ τ'-mono
  Ω-fib⊣τ' .unit {S} u' m = inc
    (_ , u' , Fib U .id , m , sym (Fib U .idr (α ·₁ u')))
  Ω-fib⊣τ' ._⊣ₚ_.counit {T} v = rec! λ η' u' w mem eq →
    subst (_∈ T) (sym eq) (T .closed mem w)
```

## The section property: equations 2.25–2.29

When $F_\alpha$ has (mere, strict) lifts — the split-fibration
situation of the paper's proof — every arrow into a reindexed object
*is* an image, so the counit inclusion becomes the *equality*
$\Omega_\alpha(\tau'_\alpha\,T) = T$ of equation 2.26:
$\tau'_\alpha$ is an injective section of the surjective
$\Omega_\alpha$, exactly the closing claim of Lemma 2.4.

```agda
module _ {U V : B.Ob} (α : B.Hom U V) where
  has-lifts : Type ℓ
  has-lifts = ∀ {η : Fib U .Ob} {ξ' : Fib V .Ob}
      (v : Fib U .Hom η (α ·₀ ξ'))
    → ∃[ η' ∈ Fib V .Ob ] Σ[ u' ∈ Fib V .Hom η' ξ' ]
        Σ[ p ∈ α ·₀ η' ≡ η ]
          (subst (λ z → Fib U .Hom z (α ·₀ ξ')) p (α ·₁ u') ≡ v)

  private
    lift-case
      : ∀ {ξ' η η'} (T : Sieve (Fib U) (α ·₀ ξ')) (u' : Fib V .Hom η' ξ')
        (p : α ·₀ η' ≡ η) (v : Fib U .Hom η (α ·₀ ξ'))
      → subst (λ z → Fib U .Hom z (α ·₀ ξ')) p (α ·₁ u') ≡ v
      → v ∈ T
      → ((α ·₁ u') ∈ T)
      × (v ≡ Fib U ._∘_ (α ·₁ u')
               (subst (Fib U .Hom _) (sym p) (Fib U .id)))
    lift-case {ξ'} {η} {η'} T u' p = J
      (λ η p → (v : Fib U .Hom η (α ·₀ ξ'))
             → subst (λ z → Fib U .Hom z (α ·₀ ξ')) p (α ·₁ u') ≡ v
             → v ∈ T
             → ((α ·₁ u') ∈ T)
             × (v ≡ Fib U ._∘_ (α ·₁ u')
                      (subst (Fib U .Hom η) (sym p) (Fib U .id))))
      (λ v peq m →
          subst (_∈ T) (sym (sym (transport-refl _) ∙ peq)) m
        , sym peq ∙ transport-refl _ ∙ sym (Fib U .idr _)
          ∙ ap (Fib U ._∘_ (α ·₁ u')) (sym (transport-refl (Fib U .id))))
      p

  lifts→section
    : has-lifts → ∀ {ξ' : Fib V .Ob} (T : Sieve (Fib U) (α ·₀ ξ'))
    → Ω[_] α (τ'[_] α T) ≡ T
  lifts→section lifts {ξ'} T = ext λ v → Ω-ua
    (Ω-fib⊣τ' α ._⊣ₚ_.counit {T} v)
    (λ m → rec!
      (λ η' u' p peq →
        let (mT , veq) = lift-case T u' p v peq m
        in inc (η' , u' , subst (Fib U .Hom _) (sym p) (Fib U .id) , mT , veq))
      (lifts v))

  lifts→τ'-injective
    : has-lifts → ∀ {ξ' : Fib V .Ob} {T T' : Sieve (Fib U) (α ·₀ ξ')}
    → τ'[_] α T ≡ τ'[_] α T' → T ≡ T'
  lifts→τ'-injective lifts {T = T} {T'} q =
    sym (lifts→section lifts T) ∙ ap (Ω[_] α) q ∙ lifts→section lifts T'

  lifts→Ω-surjective
    : has-lifts → ∀ {ξ' : Fib V .Ob} (T : Sieve (Fib U) (α ·₀ ξ'))
    → Σ[ S ∈ Sieve (Fib V) ξ' ] (Ω[_] α S ≡ T)
  lifts→Ω-surjective lifts T = τ'[_] α T , lifts→section lifts T
```
